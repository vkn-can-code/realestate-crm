# DESIGN SPECIFICATION 09: WATERFRONT / SCENIC PROPERTY

- **TEMPLATE NAME**: Waterfront / Scenic Property
- **BUSINESS PURPOSE**: Showcase coastal homes, lakefront estates, mountain retreats, and panoramic view properties.
- **TARGET AUDIENCE**: Vacation home buyers, luxury coastal investors, retiree buyers.
- **PRIMARY PLATFORM**: Instagram Portrait (4:5 / 1080x1350)
- **SECONDARY PLATFORM**: Facebook Post (1.91:1 / 1200x630)
- **VISUAL EMOTION**: Panoramic, Tranquil, Expansive, Breathtaking
- **ART DIRECTION**: Ocean panoramic aesthetic. Ultrawide 16:9 crop hero image positioned at top center, soft wave gradient background, translucent ocean blue badges.
- **LAYOUT ARCHITECTURE**: Top panoramic hero frame + floating translucent glass card below with view specs (Oceanfront Feet, Dock Access, Sunset Exposure).
- **IMAGE COMPOSITION**: Sunset waterfront exterior, infinity pool view, or mountain panorama.
- **TEXT HIERARCHY**:
  - Title: `Playfair Display` (42px, Bold 800, White `#FFFFFF`)
  - Subtitle: `Inter` (18px, Medium 500, Sky Blue `#7DD3FC`)
  - Price Tag: `Inter` (36px, ExtraBold 900, White `#FFFFFF`)
- **COLOR STRATEGY**: Ocean Deep Blue (`#0369A1`), Sky Blue (`#7DD3FC`), Sand Gold (`#FDE047`).
- **CTA STRATEGY**: "REQUEST WATERFRONT SPEC SHEET".
- **PROPERTY DATA USED**: Title, Location, Price, Waterfront Feet/View Type, Scenic Photo.
- **UNIQUE DESIGN CHARACTERISTICS**: Ultrawide 16:9 crop hero with ocean blue translucent glass specs overlay.
- **WHY THIS TEMPLATE IS DIFFERENT FROM THE OTHER 29**: Specifically engineered for scenic/waterfront photography with panoramic crop framing.

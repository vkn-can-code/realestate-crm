# DESIGN SPECIFICATION 12: JUST LISTED SHOWCASE

- **TEMPLATE NAME**: Just Listed Showcase
- **BUSINESS PURPOSE**: Announce a new active listing to the market across all social channels to drive open house visits and inquiries.
- **TARGET AUDIENCE**: Active buyers, local residents, real estate agents.
- **PRIMARY PLATFORM**: Instagram Square (1:1 / 1080x1080)
- **SECONDARY PLATFORM**: Facebook Post (1.91:1 / 1200x630)
- **VISUAL EMOTION**: Fresh, High-Impact, Exciting, Official
- **ART DIRECTION**: Modern high-contrast poster style. Top 75% full-bleed hero exterior image with an asymmetric diagonal "JUST LISTED" badge + bottom dark slate glass card holding property specs and agent contact.
- **LAYOUT ARCHITECTURE**: Full-width top hero image with bottom glass card footer.
- **IMAGE COMPOSITION**: Daylight exterior front elevation photo.
- **TEXT HIERARCHY**:
  - Badge: `Inter` (14px, Black 900, White on Blue `#2563EB`)
  - Title: `Playfair Display` (42px, Bold 800, White `#FFFFFF`)
  - Price: `Inter` (36px, ExtraBold 900, Amber `#F59E0B`)
  - Specs Bar: `Inter` (16px, Bold 700, Light Slate `#E2E8F0`)
- **COLOR STRATEGY**: Slate Charcoal (`#0F172A`), Vivid Blue (`#2563EB`), Warm Gold (`#F59E0B`).
- **CTA STRATEGY**: "LINK IN BIO FOR VIRTUAL TOUR".
- **PROPERTY DATA USED**: Title, Price, Location, Beds, Baths, Sqft, Exterior Photo, Agent Info.
- **UNIQUE DESIGN CHARACTERISTICS**: Asymmetric diagonal badge with bottom frosted glass specs panel.
- **WHY THIS TEMPLATE IS DIFFERENT FROM THE OTHER 29**: Classic US brokerage Just Listed layout with high-visibility blue badge and bottom glass bar.

# DESIGN SPECIFICATION 06: MINIMAL LUXURY PROPERTY

- **TEMPLATE NAME**: Minimal Luxury Property
- **BUSINESS PURPOSE**: Appeal to ultra-minimalist luxury buyers who appreciate clean lines, negative space, and architectural restraint.
- **TARGET AUDIENCE**: High-end minimalist buyers, architects, design purists.
- **PRIMARY PLATFORM**: Instagram Square (1:1 / 1080x1080)
- **SECONDARY PLATFORM**: LinkedIn Post (1.91:1 / 1200x627)
- **VISUAL EMOTION**: Serene, High-End, Uncluttered, Pure
- **ART DIRECTION**: Japanese/Nordic minimalist aesthetic. 70% negative space with a single offset rectangular photo frame, hairline slate border, and light gray typography.
- **LAYOUT ARCHITECTURE**: Offset right photo frame occupying 50% width and 80% height, left column dedicated to vertical text stack.
- **IMAGE COMPOSITION**: Clean exterior architectural facade with neutral sky or interior living area with minimalist furniture.
- **TEXT HIERARCHY**:
  - Title: `Inter` (32px, Medium 500, Charcoal `#0F172A`, Letter spacing 1px)
  - Price: `Inter` (24px, Bold 700, Slate `#475569`)
  - Location: `Inter` (14px, Regular 400, Muted Slate `#94A3B8`)
- **COLOR STRATEGY**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), Soft Slate (`#F1F5F9`), Dark Slate (`#0F172A`).
- **CTA STRATEGY**: Minimalist arrow link (`INQUIRE →`).
- **PROPERTY DATA USED**: Title, Price, Location, Single Hero Photo.
- **UNIQUE DESIGN CHARACTERISTICS**: Extreme negative space (70% white space) with offset vertical text column.
- **WHY THIS TEMPLATE IS DIFFERENT FROM THE OTHER 29**: It is the only template engineered with 70% white space and offset typography.

# DESIGN SPECIFICATION 19: TOP PRODUCER

- **TEMPLATE NAME**: Top Producer Award Announcement
- **BUSINESS PURPOSE**: Highlight brokerage award recognition (Top 1% Agent, President's Circle, Producer of the Year).
- **TARGET AUDIENCE**: High-end sellers, industry peers.
- **PRIMARY PLATFORM**: Instagram Story (9:16 / 1080x1920)
- **SECONDARY PLATFORM**: Facebook Post (1.91:1 / 1200x630)
- **VISUAL EMOTION**: Elite, Authoritative, Unmatched Excellence
- **ART DIRECTION**: Forbes/Fortune ranking style. Full-height dark navy background with double-ring gold crest, bold serif headline, and centered portrait.
- **LAYOUT ARCHITECTURE**: Vertical center stack with gold double-ring framing and award title banner.
- **IMAGE COMPOSITION**: Formal agent portrait.
- **TEXT HIERARCHY**:
  - Award Rank: `Playfair Display` (36px, Bold 800, Gold `#D97706`)
  - Agent Name: `Inter` (32px, Black 900, White `#FFFFFF`)
- **COLOR STRATEGY**: Deep Slate (`#0F172A`), Amber Gold (`#D97706`), Soft White.
- **CTA STRATEGY**: "WORK WITH A TOP 1% REALTOR".
- **PROPERTY DATA USED**: Agent Name, Award Title, Brokerage Name, Agent Photo.
- **UNIQUE DESIGN CHARACTERISTICS**: Double-ring gold crest frame surrounding the agent portrait.
- **WHY THIS TEMPLATE IS DIFFERENT FROM THE OTHER 29**: Formal ranking poster template designed around top producer honors.

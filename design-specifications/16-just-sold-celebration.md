# DESIGN SPECIFICATION 16: JUST SOLD CELEBRATION

- **TEMPLATE NAME**: Just Sold Celebration
- **BUSINESS PURPOSE**: Showcase successful closing, build seller trust, and generate new listing leads.
- **TARGET AUDIENCE**: Homeowners considering selling in the next 3-6 months.
- **PRIMARY PLATFORM**: Instagram Square (1:1 / 1080x1080)
- **SECONDARY PLATFORM**: Facebook Post (1.91:1 / 1200x630)
- **VISUAL EMOTION**: Celebratory, Victorious, High-Performing, Authoritative
- **ART DIRECTION**: Trophy celebration style. Deep emerald green backdrop (`#064E3B`) with gold trophy icon, large "JUST SOLD" headline, and 3 key success metrics (% Over Asking, Days on Market, Total Offer Count).
- **LAYOUT ARCHITECTURE**: Top hero photo with bottom emerald green card displaying 3 metric circles.
- **IMAGE COMPOSITION**: Sold house exterior with "SOLD" yard sign.
- **TEXT HIERARCHY**:
  - Main Headline: `Playfair Display` (46px, Bold 800, Gold `#F59E0B`)
  - Success Metric Numbers: `Inter` (32px, Black 900, White `#FFFFFF`)
  - Sub-label: `Inter` (14px, Medium 500, Mint `#A7F3D0`)
- **COLOR STRATEGY**: Deep Emerald (`#064E3B`), Gold `#F59E0B`, Mint `#A7F3D0`.
- **CTA STRATEGY**: "WANT TO KNOW WHAT YOUR HOME IS WORTH?".
- **PROPERTY DATA USED**: Title, Final Sold Price, % Over Asking, Days on Market, Photo.
- **UNIQUE DESIGN CHARACTERISTICS**: Emerald green background with 3 circular sales performance metric callouts.
- **WHY THIS TEMPLATE IS DIFFERENT FROM THE OTHER 29**: It is the only template highlighting sales performance stats (% over asking price).

# DESIGN SPECIFICATION 15: UNDER CONTRACT PENDING

- **TEMPLATE NAME**: Under Contract / Pending Sale Announcement
- **BUSINESS PURPOSE**: Build agent authority and market momentum by demonstrating fast listing movement.
- **TARGET AUDIENCE**: Prospective home sellers in the neighborhood.
- **PRIMARY PLATFORM**: Instagram Story (9:16 / 1080x1920)
- **SECONDARY PLATFORM**: Instagram Square (1:1 / 1080x1080)
- **VISUAL EMOTION**: Successful, In-Demand, Active, Professional
- **ART DIRECTION**: Stamp/Seal overlay style. Center property photo overlaid with a large diagonal "UNDER CONTRACT" rubber stamp graphic.
- **LAYOUT ARCHITECTURE**: Full-bleed photo with diagonal translucent stamp badge overlay and bottom backup offer note.
- **IMAGE COMPOSITION**: Exterior listing photo.
- **TEXT HIERARCHY**:
  - Stamp Text: `Inter` (28px, Black 900, Uppercase, Amber Gold `#F59E0B`, Rotated -15deg)
  - Days on Market: `Inter` (18px, Bold 700, White `#FFFFFF`)
- **COLOR STRATEGY**: Dark Slate (`#0F172A`), Amber Gold (`#F59E0B`), White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **CTA STRATEGY**: "ACCEPTING BACKUP OFFERS — CONTACT LISTING AGENT".
- **PROPERTY DATA USED**: Title, Days on Market, Property Photo.
- **UNIQUE DESIGN CHARACTERISTICS**: Diagonal rubber-stamp badge overlay set at -15 degrees angle.
- **WHY THIS TEMPLATE IS DIFFERENT FROM THE OTHER 29**: Uses a diagonal stamp graphic overlay signifying contract status.

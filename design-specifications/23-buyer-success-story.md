# DESIGN SPECIFICATION 23: BUYER SUCCESS STORY

- **TEMPLATE NAME**: Key Handover Buyer Success Story
- **BUSINESS PURPOSE**: Celebrate first-time buyers or relocation clients receiving their keys to inspire future buyers.
- **TARGET AUDIENCE**: First-time homebuyers, renters looking to buy.
- **PRIMARY PLATFORM**: Instagram Square (1:1 / 1080x1080)
- **SECONDARY PLATFORM**: Facebook Post (1.91:1 / 1200x630)
- **VISUAL EMOTION**: Joyful, Emotional, Inspiring, Celebratory
- **ART DIRECTION**: Storybook photo feature. Large lifestyle photo of happy buyers outside their home, topped with a "CONGRATULATIONS!" banner and a short story summary card below.
- **LAYOUT ARCHITECTURE**: Top 65% photo of buyers holding keys + bottom 35% story card detailing how the agent won their dream home in a competitive market.
- **IMAGE COMPOSITION**: Lifestyle photo of buyers holding keys in front of their new home.
- **TEXT HIERARCHY**:
  - Banner: `Inter` (14px, Black 900, Emerald Green `#10B981`)
  - Headline: `Playfair Display` (34px, Bold 800, White `#FFFFFF`)
  - Story Summary: `Inter` (15px, Regular 400, Light Gray `#CBD5E1`)
- **COLOR STRATEGY**: Warm Slate (`#1E293B`), Emerald Green (`#10B981`), Gold Accent.
- **CTA STRATEGY**: "LET US HELP YOU FIND YOUR DREAM HOME".
- **PROPERTY DATA USED**: Buyer Names, Story Paragraph, Key Handover Photo, Location.
- **UNIQUE DESIGN CHARACTERISTICS**: Emotional human-interest layout featuring buyers holding keys outside their home.
- **WHY THIS TEMPLATE IS DIFFERENT FROM THE OTHER 29**: Focuses on the emotional human story of key handover day rather than property specs.

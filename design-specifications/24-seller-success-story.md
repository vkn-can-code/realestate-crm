# DESIGN SPECIFICATION 24: SELLER SUCCESS STORY

- **TEMPLATE NAME**: Sold Over Asking Seller Success Story
- **BUSINESS PURPOSE**: Demonstrate marketing strategy results (% over asking, days on market) to win listing presentations.
- **TARGET AUDIENCE**: Homeowners looking to sell for maximum return.
- **PRIMARY PLATFORM**: LinkedIn Post (1.91:1 / 1200x627)
- **SECONDARY PLATFORM**: Instagram Square (1:1 / 1080x1080)
- **VISUAL EMOTION**: Results-Driven, Strategic, High-Return, Expert
- **ART DIRECTION**: Executive case study style. Left 50% = Sold Property Photo with "SOLD IN 5 DAYS" sash, Right 50% = 3 Key Seller Metrics (List Price vs Sold Price, Total Offers Received, Marketing Impressions).
- **LAYOUT ARCHITECTURE**: Horizontal 50/50 split showing property photo on left and financial return metrics on right.
- **IMAGE COMPOSITION**: Exterior photo of sold home.
- **TEXT HIERARCHY**:
  - Over-Asking Stat: `Inter` (42px, Black 900, Green `#22C55E`)
  - Strategy Headline: `Playfair Display` (28px, Bold 700, White `#FFFFFF`)
- **COLOR STRATEGY**: Dark Slate (`#0F172A`), Success Green (`#22C55E`), White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **CTA STRATEGY**: "REQUEST FREE HOME VALUATION & MARKETING PLAN".
- **PROPERTY DATA USED**: List Price, Sold Price, % Difference, Days on Market, Offer Count, Photo.
- **UNIQUE DESIGN CHARACTERISTICS**: Side-by-side case study format comparing list price vs final sold price.
- **WHY THIS TEMPLATE IS DIFFERENT FROM THE OTHER 29**: It is the only seller case study displaying list price vs sold price return metrics side-by-side.

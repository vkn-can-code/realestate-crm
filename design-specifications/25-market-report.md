# DESIGN SPECIFICATION 25: MARKET REPORT

- **TEMPLATE NAME**: Local Real Estate Market Update Infographic
- **BUSINESS PURPOSE**: Establish market expertise by presenting quarterly median home prices, inventory, and mortgage trend data.
- **TARGET AUDIENCE**: Buyers, sellers, homeowners tracking local equity.
- **PRIMARY PLATFORM**: Instagram Square (1:1 / 1080x1080)
- **SECONDARY PLATFORM**: LinkedIn Post (1.91:1 / 1200x627)
- **VISUAL EMOTION**: Authoritative, Educational, Data-Rich, Insightful
- **ART DIRECTION**: Modern 4-quadrant infographic. 4 distinct metric tiles displaying: 1. Median Home Price (+$25k), 2. Average Days on Market (18 Days), 3. Total Inventory (+12%), 4. Mortgage Rate Trend (6.2%).
- **LAYOUT ARCHITECTURE**: 2x2 grid matrix of data tiles with header title bar and bottom market summary note.
- **IMAGE COMPOSITION**: Minimal background skyline graphic or high-level neighborhood vista with dark overlay.
- **TEXT HIERARCHY**:
  - Metric Numbers: `Inter` (38px, Black 900, Electric Blue `#38BDF8`)
  - Metric Labels: `Inter` (13px, SemiBold 600, Uppercase, Gray `#94A3B8`)
- **COLOR STRATEGY**: Dark Navy (`#0F172A`), Sky Blue (`#38BDF8`), Mint Green (`#34D399`).
- **CTA STRATEGY**: "WANT A CUSTOM MARKET REPORT FOR YOUR NEIGHBORHOOD?".
- **PROPERTY DATA USED**: City/ZIP Code, Median Price, Days on Market, Inventory Count, Interest Rate.
- **UNIQUE DESIGN CHARACTERISTICS**: 4-quadrant data visualization matrix with numerical metric callouts.
- **WHY THIS TEMPLATE IS DIFFERENT FROM THE OTHER 29**: It is the only 4-quadrant infographic template built for market statistical metrics.

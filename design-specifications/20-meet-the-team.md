# DESIGN SPECIFICATION 20: MEET THE TEAM

- **TEMPLATE NAME**: Brokerage Team Spotlight
- **BUSINESS PURPOSE**: Showcase the collective strength, diverse roles, and collaborative power of a real estate group.
- **TARGET AUDIENCE**: Luxury buyers, commercial clients, large sellers.
- **PRIMARY PLATFORM**: Facebook Post (1.91:1 / 1200x630)
- **SECONDARY PLATFORM**: Instagram Square (1:1 / 1080x1080)
- **VISUAL EMOTION**: Strong, Collaborative, Capable, Unified
- **ART DIRECTION**: Modern corporate team grid. 3 or 4 circular avatar headshots arranged horizontally over a dark slate background with team motto banner.
- **LAYOUT ARCHITECTURE**: Top team motto banner + middle 4-avatar portrait row + bottom brokerage credentials.
- **IMAGE COMPOSITION**: Team photo or 4 individual agent headshots.
- **TEXT HIERARCHY**:
  - Team Name: `Playfair Display` (44px, Bold 800, White `#FFFFFF`)
  - Subtitle: `Inter` (18px, SemiBold 600, Blue `#60A5FA`)
- **COLOR STRATEGY**: Dark Slate (`#0F172A`), Corporate Blue (`#3B82F6`), White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **CTA STRATEGY**: "OUR TEAM IS READY TO SERVE YOU".
- **PROPERTY DATA USED**: Team Name, Member Names, Roles, Group Photo/Headshots.
- **UNIQUE DESIGN CHARACTERISTICS**: Multi-avatar horizontal row layout showing 3-4 team members simultaneously.
- **WHY THIS TEMPLATE IS DIFFERENT FROM THE OTHER 29**: It is the only multi-person team grid template in the library.

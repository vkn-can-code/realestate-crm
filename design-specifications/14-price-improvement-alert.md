# DESIGN SPECIFICATION 14: PRICE IMPROVEMENT ALERT

- **TEMPLATE NAME**: Price Improvement Alert
- **BUSINESS PURPOSE**: Re-engage buyers who previously viewed a property by announcing a fresh price drop or price reduction.
- **TARGET AUDIENCE**: Price-sensitive buyers, previously interested leads, buyer agents.
- **PRIMARY PLATFORM**: Instagram Square (1:1 / 1080x1080)
- **SECONDARY PLATFORM**: Facebook Feed (1.91:1 / 1200x630)
- **VISUAL EMOTION**: High-Impact, Urgent, Opportunity, Value
- **ART DIRECTION**: High-visibility alert style. Deep amber/rust backdrop (`#78350F`) with a flame red price drop badge, crossed-out original price, and large bold new price display.
- **LAYOUT ARCHITECTURE**: Top hero photo with diagonal slash line across price block showing Old Price -> New Price.
- **IMAGE COMPOSITION**: High-resolution exterior or key feature photo.
- **TEXT HIERARCHY**:
  - Alert Badge: `Inter` (14px, Black 900, Crimson `#EF4444`)
  - Original Price: `Inter` (22px, Line-Through, Muted Gray `#9CA3AF`)
  - New Price: `Inter` (44px, Black 900, Bright Yellow `#FBBF24`)
- **COLOR STRATEGY**: Rust Brown (`#78350F`), Flame Red (`#EF4444`), Bright Yellow (`#FBBF24`).
- **CTA STRATEGY**: "SUBMIT OFFER AT NEW PRICE".
- **PROPERTY DATA USED**: Title, Original Price, New Price, Price Reduction Amount, Photo.
- **UNIQUE DESIGN CHARACTERISTICS**: Crossed-out original price typography paired with a bright yellow new price display.
- **WHY THIS TEMPLATE IS DIFFERENT FROM THE OTHER 29**: It is the only template displaying a strikethrough original price comparison.

# DESIGN SPECIFICATION 11: COMING SOON VIP TEASER

- **TEMPLATE NAME**: Coming Soon VIP Teaser
- **BUSINESS PURPOSE**: Build anticipation and generate pre-market VIP buyer leads before official MLS launch.
- **TARGET AUDIENCE**: Hot buyers, local agents, VIP client list.
- **PRIMARY PLATFORM**: Instagram Story (9:16 / 1080x1920)
- **SECONDARY PLATFORM**: Instagram Square (1:1 / 1080x1080)
- **VISUAL EMOTION**: Intriguing, Exclusive, Secret, VIP
- **ART DIRECTION**: Dark vignette aesthetic. Dimmed property photo with keyhole/vignette shadow, purple VIP ribbon badge, countdown indicator, and "OFF-MARKET" keyline lockup.
- **LAYOUT ARCHITECTURE**: Full-bleed darkened photo with center VIP lockup badge and bottom registration button.
- **IMAGE COMPOSITION**: Exterior photo dimmed to 60% brightness with moody lighting.
- **TEXT HIERARCHY**:
  - VIP Lockup: `Inter` (14px, Bold 800, Uppercase, Purple `#A855F7` on Glass)
  - Headline: `Playfair Display` (46px, Bold 800, White `#FFFFFF`)
  - Launch Date: `Inter` (18px, SemiBold 600, Light Purple `#E9D5FF`)
- **COLOR STRATEGY**: Midnight Violet (`#1E1B4B`), Electric Purple (`#A855F7`), White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **CTA STRATEGY**: "REGISTER FOR EARLY ACCESS SHOWING".
- **PROPERTY DATA USED**: Neighborhood, Estimated Price Range, Launch Date, Dimmed Exterior Photo.
- **UNIQUE DESIGN CHARACTERISTICS**: Dimmed photo with vignette overlay and purple VIP keyline badge.
- **WHY THIS TEMPLATE IS DIFFERENT FROM THE OTHER 29**: Uses a vignette photo overlay with a purple off-market VIP access aesthetic.

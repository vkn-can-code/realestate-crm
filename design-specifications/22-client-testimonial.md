# DESIGN SPECIFICATION 22: CLIENT TESTIMONIAL

- **TEMPLATE NAME**: 5-Star Client Review Card
- **BUSINESS PURPOSE**: Build trust, social proof, and credibility using verified client feedback.
- **TARGET AUDIENCE**: Prospective buyers and sellers evaluating agent reputation.
- **PRIMARY PLATFORM**: Instagram Square (1:1 / 1080x1080)
- **SECONDARY PLATFORM**: Facebook Post (1.91:1 / 1200x630)
- **VISUAL EMOTION**: Authentic, Trustworthy, Satisfied, High-Rating
- **ART DIRECTION**: Editorial quote style. Floating dark glass container with giant vector quotation mark (`“`), 5 gold review stars, italicized quote text, client attribution, and inset property thumbnail.
- **LAYOUT ARCHITECTURE**: Top 5-star rating badge + middle large quote paragraph + bottom client name & property thumbnail photo.
- **IMAGE COMPOSITION**: Small circular or rounded square thumbnail of the purchased/sold home.
- **TEXT HIERARCHY**:
  - Quote Paragraph: `Playfair Display` (26px, Italic 500, White `#F8FAFC`, Line Height 1.5)
  - Client Name: `Inter` (18px, Bold 700, Cyan `#38BDF8`)
  - Location/Transaction: `Inter` (14px, Medium 500, Gray `#94A3B8`)
- **COLOR STRATEGY**: Dark Slate (`#0F172A`), Yellow Stars (`#FBBF24`), Cyan Accent (`#38BDF8`).
- **CTA STRATEGY**: "READ MORE 5-STAR REVIEWS ON ZILLOW & GOOGLE".
- **PROPERTY DATA USED**: Client Quote, Rating, Client Name, Transaction Type, Purchased Property Photo.
- **UNIQUE DESIGN CHARACTERISTICS**: Large graphic quotation marks paired with 5 gold rating stars and italic editorial quote styling.
- **WHY THIS TEMPLATE IS DIFFERENT FROM THE OTHER 29**: It is the only template specifically designed around customer quote typography and 5-star ratings.

# DESIGN SPECIFICATION 13: OPEN HOUSE EVENT BANNER

- **TEMPLATE NAME**: Open House Event Invitation Banner
- **BUSINESS PURPOSE**: Drive physical foot traffic to weekend open house events.
- **TARGET AUDIENCE**: Local buyers, neighborhood foot traffic, buyer agents.
- **PRIMARY PLATFORM**: Instagram Square (1:1 / 1080x1080)
- **SECONDARY PLATFORM**: Instagram Story (9:16 / 1080x1920)
- **VISUAL EMOTION**: Welcoming, Clear, Actionable, Event-Focused
- **ART DIRECTION**: Architectural event invitation style. Top 55% hero photo frame + middle vibrant green event ribbon ("OPEN HOUSE THIS SATURDAY 2-4 PM") + bottom location map pin & address card.
- **LAYOUT ARCHITECTURE**: Stacked 3-part layout: Photo -> Event Banner -> Address & Directions Card.
- **IMAGE COMPOSITION**: Inviting front entrance or open living space photo.
- **TEXT HIERARCHY**:
  - Event Banner: `Inter` (20px, ExtraBold 900, Uppercase, Emerald Green `#10B981`)
  - Date & Time: `Inter` (24px, Black 900, White `#FFFFFF`)
  - Address: `Inter` (18px, SemiBold 600, Sky Blue `#38BDF8`)
- **COLOR STRATEGY**: Dark Slate (`#0F172A`), Emerald Green (`#10B981`), Sky Blue (`#38BDF8`).
- **CTA STRATEGY**: "TAP FOR MAP DIRECTIONS".
- **PROPERTY DATA USED**: Event Date, Time Window, Property Address, Title, Photo.
- **UNIQUE DESIGN CHARACTERISTICS**: Dedicated 3-part stacked layout featuring a prominent green date/time ribbon and map pin icon.
- **WHY THIS TEMPLATE IS DIFFERENT FROM THE OTHER 29**: It is the only template specifically formatted with an event date/time banner and map directions.

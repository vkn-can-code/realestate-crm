# DESIGN SPECIFICATION 10: INVESTMENT PROPERTY

- **TEMPLATE NAME**: Investment Property ROI Showcase
- **BUSINESS PURPOSE**: Attract real estate investors and landlords by highlighting cap rates, rental yield, and cash flow potential.
- **TARGET AUDIENCE**: Real estate investors, portfolio managers, 1031 exchange buyers.
- **PRIMARY PLATFORM**: LinkedIn Post (1.91:1 / 1200x627)
- **SECONDARY PLATFORM**: Instagram Square (1:1 / 1080x1080)
- **VISUAL EMOTION**: Data-Driven, Lucrative, Analytical, Trustworthy
- **ART DIRECTION**: Financial dashboard aesthetic. Dark navy background with 3 high-contrast financial metric boxes (Estimated Cap Rate, Monthly Cashflow, Occupancy Rate).
- **LAYOUT ARCHITECTURE**: Left 40% = Property Photo + Location, Right 60% = Financial Metrics Matrix & ROI Calculator Breakdown.
- **IMAGE COMPOSITION**: Multi-family unit facade or turnkey residential exterior.
- **TEXT HIERARCHY**:
  - Cap Rate Metric: `Inter` (44px, Black 900, Emerald Green `#10B981`)
  - Monthly Cashflow: `Inter` (32px, ExtraBold 800, Emerald Green `#34D399`)
  - Title: `Inter` (28px, Bold 700, White `#FFFFFF`)
- **COLOR STRATEGY**: Slate Navy (`#0F172A`), Emerald Financial Green (`#10B981`), Light Mint (`#A7F3D0`).
- **CTA STRATEGY**: "REQUEST PRO-FORMA FINANCIAL MODEL".
- **PROPERTY DATA USED**: Title, Purchase Price, Estimated Rental Income, Cap Rate, Property Photo.
- **UNIQUE DESIGN CHARACTERISTICS**: Financial matrix layout displaying Cap Rate and cash flow metrics alongside property photo.
- **WHY THIS TEMPLATE IS DIFFERENT FROM THE OTHER 29**: It is the only investor-focused template built around cap rate metrics and cash flow calculations.

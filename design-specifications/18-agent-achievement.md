# DESIGN SPECIFICATION 18: AGENT ACHIEVEMENT

- **TEMPLATE NAME**: Agent Career Achievement Showcase
- **BUSINESS PURPOSE**: Celebrate personal sales milestones, volume achievements, or industry awards.
- **TARGET AUDIENCE**: Past clients, professional network, potential sellers.
- **PRIMARY PLATFORM**: Instagram Square (1:1 / 1080x1080)
- **SECONDARY PLATFORM**: LinkedIn Post (1.91:1 / 1200x627)
- **VISUAL EMOTION**: Successful, Prestigious, Accomplished
- **ART DIRECTION**: Golden trophy style. Dark obsidian background with golden award seal graphic, giant volume stat ($50M+ Sold), and agent headshot overlay.
- **LAYOUT ARCHITECTURE**: Top golden crest graphic + center giant stat metric + bottom agent portrait lockup.
- **IMAGE COMPOSITION**: Professional agent portrait.
- **TEXT HIERARCHY**:
  - Achievement Stat: `Inter` (64px, Black 900, Gold `#F59E0B`)
  - Award Title: `Playfair Display` (28px, Bold 700, White `#FFFFFF`)
- **COLOR STRATEGY**: Obsidian (`#020617`), Gold (`#F59E0B`), White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **CTA STRATEGY**: "THANK YOU TO OUR VALUED CLIENTS & PARTNERS".
- **PROPERTY DATA USED**: Agent Name, Sales Volume Stat, Year, Headshot Photo.
- **UNIQUE DESIGN CHARACTERISTICS**: Large golden achievement stat ($50M+) with award crest graphics.
- **WHY THIS TEMPLATE IS DIFFERENT FROM THE OTHER 29**: It is the only template centered on personal sales volume statistics and golden award crests.

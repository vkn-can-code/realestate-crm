# DESIGN SPECIFICATION 21: BROKERAGE MILESTONE

- **TEMPLATE NAME**: Brokerage Expansion & Milestone
- **BUSINESS PURPOSE**: Announce company milestones ($1 Billion Sold, New Office Opening, 10-Year Anniversary).
- **TARGET AUDIENCE**: Community, clients, industry partners, prospective recruit agents.
- **PRIMARY PLATFORM**: LinkedIn Post (1.91:1 / 1200x627)
- **SECONDARY PLATFORM**: Instagram Square (1:1 / 1080x1080)
- **VISUAL EMOTION**: Established, Growing, Powerful, Community Pillar
- **ART DIRECTION**: Corporate press release aesthetic. Dark background with city skyline silhouette background overlay, bold center headline, and brokerage crest logo.
- **LAYOUT ARCHITECTURE**: Top brokerage logo crest + center giant headline + bottom thank-you signature block.
- **IMAGE COMPOSITION**: City skyline photo with dark gradient overlay.
- **TEXT HIERARCHY**:
  - Milestone Stat: `Inter` (56px, Black 900, White `#FFFFFF`)
  - Announcement: `Playfair Display` (32px, Bold 700, Gold `#F59E0B`)
- **COLOR STRATEGY**: Corporate Navy (`#1E293B`), Sky Blue (`#38BDF8`), Gold (`#F59E0B`).
- **CTA STRATEGY**: "DISCOVER THE REALTY PULSE DIFFERENCE".
- **PROPERTY DATA USED**: Brokerage Name, Milestone Text/Stat, Logo Graphic, Skyline Background.
- **UNIQUE DESIGN CHARACTERISTICS**: City skyline background silhouette paired with corporate press release typography.
- **WHY THIS TEMPLATE IS DIFFERENT FROM THE OTHER 29**: It is the only corporate press release layout with a city skyline background.

# DESIGN SPECIFICATION 29: NEIGHBORHOOD GUIDE

- **TEMPLATE NAME**: Local Neighborhood & Community Spotlight
- **BUSINESS PURPOSE**: Position the agent as a local neighborhood expert by showcasing school ratings, dining, and community parks.
- **TARGET AUDIENCE**: Relocation buyers, out-of-state buyers, families.
- **PRIMARY PLATFORM**: Instagram Square (1:1 / 1080x1080)
- **SECONDARY PLATFORM**: Pinterest Pin (2:3 / 1000x1500)
- **VISUAL EMOTION**: Vibrant, Community-Oriented, Welcoming, Local
- **ART DIRECTION**: Travel & lifestyle magazine style. Top 60% = Iconic neighborhood streetscape/park photo with location tag badge + bottom 40% = 3 Community Highlights (Top Rated Schools: 9/10, Walk Score: 88, Local Dining: 40+ Cafes).
- **LAYOUT ARCHITECTURE**: Top streetscape photo with bottom community amenity stat pills.
- **IMAGE COMPOSITION**: Neighborhood streetscape, park, or downtown dining strip photo.
- **TEXT HIERARCHY**:
  - Neighborhood Name: `Playfair Display` (42px, Bold 800, White `#FFFFFF`)
  - Highlight Pills: `Inter` (14px, Bold 700, White on Glass)
- **COLOR STRATEGY**: Slate Blue (`#1E293B`), Emerald (`#10B981`), Amber (`#F59E0B`).
- **CTA STRATEGY**: "DOWNLOAD OUR COMPLETE NEIGHBORHOOD GUIDE".
- **PROPERTY DATA USED**: Neighborhood Name, School Rating, Walk Score, Local Highlights, Street Photo.
- **UNIQUE DESIGN CHARACTERISTICS**: Community scorecards displaying Walk Score and School Ratings over neighborhood photography.
- **WHY THIS TEMPLATE IS DIFFERENT FROM THE OTHER 29**: Focuses on neighborhood lifestyle, school ratings, and walk scores rather than property listings.

# DESIGN SPECIFICATION 28: MORTGAGE FINANCING

- **TEMPLATE NAME**: Fixed vs Adjustable Rate Mortgage Explainer
- **BUSINESS PURPOSE**: Demystify complex mortgage financing options and interest rates for home buyers.
- **TARGET AUDIENCE**: Active buyers concerned about interest rates and monthly payments.
- **PRIMARY PLATFORM**: Instagram Square (1:1 / 1080x1080)
- **SECONDARY PLATFORM**: LinkedIn Post (1.91:1 / 1200x627)
- **VISUAL EMOTION**: Informative, Reassuring, Financial, Clear
- **ART DIRECTION**: Financial comparison card style. Two contrasting side-by-side vertical cards comparing 30-Year Fixed vs 7/1 ARM rates, monthly payment estimates, and pros/cons.
- **LAYOUT ARCHITECTURE**: Dual vertical card comparison layout with green vs blue accent headers.
- **IMAGE COMPOSITION**: Clean financial background graphic or architectural keyframe.
- **TEXT HIERARCHY**:
  - Mortgage Rate: `Inter` (38px, Black 900, Green `#10B981`)
  - Loan Option Title: `Inter` (20px, Bold 700, White `#FFFFFF`)
- **COLOR STRATEGY**: Dark Blue (`#0F172A`), Emerald Green (`#10B981`), Royal Blue (`#2563EB`).
- **CTA STRATEGY**: "CONNECT WITH OUR PREFERRED MORTGAGE ADVISOR".
- **PROPERTY DATA USED**: Interest Rate %, Loan Types, Payment Comparison Table.
- **UNIQUE DESIGN CHARACTERISTICS**: Dual side-by-side financial comparison cards comparing mortgage rates.
- **WHY THIS TEMPLATE IS DIFFERENT FROM THE OTHER 29**: It is the only template engineered specifically for mortgage rate financial comparisons.

# DESIGN SPECIFICATION 08: NEW CONSTRUCTION

- **TEMPLATE NAME**: New Construction Launch
- **BUSINESS PURPOSE**: Promote pre-construction developments, newly built residences, and custom builder properties.
- **TARGET AUDIENCE**: New construction buyers, investors, customized home seekers.
- **PRIMARY PLATFORM**: Instagram Square (1:1 / 1080x1080)
- **SECONDARY PLATFORM**: Facebook Feed (1.91:1 / 1200x630)
- **VISUAL EMOTION**: Innovative, Forward-Looking, Fresh, Blueprint-Accurate
- **ART DIRECTION**: Architectural blueprint aesthetic. Dark navy background with cyan keyline grids, blueprint corner markers, and floor plan inlay.
- **LAYOUT ARCHITECTURE**: Top rendering image container + bottom floor plan schematic overlay card.
- **IMAGE COMPOSITION**: 3D architectural rendering or exterior facade + floor plan graphic.
- **TEXT HIERARCHY**:
  - Badge: `Inter` (12px, Bold 700, Uppercase, Cyan `#38BDF8`)
  - Title: `Inter` (36px, ExtraBold 900, White `#FFFFFF`)
  - Delivery Date: `Inter` (16px, SemiBold 600, Light Cyan `#7DD3FC`)
- **COLOR STRATEGY**: Blueprint Blue (`#0C4A6E`), Electric Cyan (`#38BDF8`), White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **CTA STRATEGY**: "DOWNLOAD FLOOR PLANS & PRICING".
- **PROPERTY DATA USED**: Development Title, Starting Price, Completion Date, Render Photo.
- **UNIQUE DESIGN CHARACTERISTICS**: Blueprint blueprint grid aesthetics with floor plan inlay card.
- **WHY THIS TEMPLATE IS DIFFERENT FROM THE OTHER 29**: It is the only architectural blueprint themed layout for new construction developments.

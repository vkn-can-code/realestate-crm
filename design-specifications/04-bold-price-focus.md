# DESIGN SPECIFICATION 04: BOLD PRICE FOCUS

- **TEMPLATE NAME**: Bold Price Focus
- **BUSINESS PURPOSE**: Drive immediate buyer inquiries by highlighting an attractive price point or competitive price positioning.
- **TARGET AUDIENCE**: Active home searchers, bargain hunters, investors.
- **PRIMARY PLATFORM**: Instagram Square (1:1 / 1080x1080)
- **SECONDARY PLATFORM**: Facebook Feed (1.91:1 / 1200x630)
- **VISUAL EMOTION**: Urgent, High-Impact, Direct, Value-Driven
- **ART DIRECTION**: High-contrast typography-first aesthetic. Giant price number occupying 35% of the poster height, backed by a diagonal contrast ribbon and inset photo window.
- **LAYOUT ARCHITECTURE**: Top 60% dedicated to massive price typography over dark background, bottom 40% dedicated to horizontal photo strip with address overlay.
- **IMAGE COMPOSITION**: Inset horizontal photo strip showing property facade.
- **TEXT HIERARCHY**:
  - Main Price: `Inter` (72px, Black 900, Neon Amber `#FBBF24`)
  - Sub-Price / Market Note: `Inter` (18px, Bold 700, White `#FFFFFF`)
  - Title: `Inter` (32px, ExtraBold 800, White `#FFFFFF`)
- **COLOR STRATEGY**: Dark Amber Rust (`#451A03`), Neon Amber (`#FBBF24`), Crimson Red (`#DC2626`).
- **CTA STRATEGY**: "SUBMIT YOUR OFFER NOW".
- **PROPERTY DATA USED**: Price, Title, Location, Exterior Photo.
- **UNIQUE DESIGN CHARACTERISTICS**: Typography-first layout where price font size (72px) dominates the composition.
- **WHY THIS TEMPLATE IS DIFFERENT FROM THE OTHER 29**: It is the only price-first template where numerical price typography takes up 35% of the visual space.

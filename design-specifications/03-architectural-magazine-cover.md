# DESIGN SPECIFICATION 03: ARCHITECTURAL MAGAZINE COVER

- **TEMPLATE NAME**: Architectural Magazine Cover
- **BUSINESS PURPOSE**: Position premier architectural listings as cover-worthy works of art.
- **TARGET AUDIENCE**: Architecture enthusiasts, high-end buyers, design-conscious clients.
- **PRIMARY PLATFORM**: Instagram Story (9:16 / 1080x1920)
- **SECONDARY PLATFORM**: Instagram Portrait (4:5 / 1080x1350)
- **VISUAL EMOTION**: Artistic, Dramatic, High-End, Architectural
- **ART DIRECTION**: Architectural Journal / Dwell Cover style. Massive hero title running across top border with overlapping photo frame and vertical side typography.
- **LAYOUT ARCHITECTURE**: Full-height vertical crop with top header title ("REALTY PULSE ARCHITECTURE"), left-aligned vertical orientation text ("VOLUME 24 // ISSUE 08"), and bottom price overlay.
- **IMAGE COMPOSITION**: Exterior architectural angle showing sharp lines, glass windows, or cantilevered roofs.
- **TEXT HIERARCHY**:
  - Cover Masthead: `Inter` (28px, Black 900, Uppercase, Letter Spacing 10px)
  - Issue Stamp: `Inter` (12px, SemiBold 600, Uppercase, Vertical Rotate -90deg)
  - Main Title: `Playfair Display` (52px, Regular 400, White `#FFFFFF`)
- **COLOR STRATEGY**: Charcoal Navy (`#090D16`), Muted Gold (`#D4AF37`), Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **CTA STRATEGY**: "SWIPE UP FOR ARCHITECTURAL TOUR".
- **PROPERTY DATA USED**: Title, Architectural Style, Location, Exterior Hero Photo.
- **UNIQUE DESIGN CHARACTERISTICS**: Vertical side text, magazine masthead top bar, minimal data density focused on pure visual aesthetics.
- **WHY THIS TEMPLATE IS DIFFERENT FROM THE OTHER 29**: Uses a vertical magazine masthead layout with rotated side metadata.

# Typography Rules & Hierarchy for Real Estate Creatives

## 1. Typeface Pairings
- **Luxury / Exclusive Estates**: `Playfair Display` (Serif) for Headlines + `Inter` (Sans-Serif) for Specs & Body.
- **Modern / Urban Condos**: `Inter` (ExtraBold/Black 800-900) for Headlines + `Montserrat` for Badges.
- **Minimalist Architecture**: `Inter` (Medium/Bold 600-700) with generous letter-spacing (`letter-spacing: 0.05em`).

## 2. Type Size Hierarchy (Based on 1080x1080 canvas)
- **Primary Headline**: 40px – 56px (Bold 800/900)
- **Price Tag**: 32px – 44px (ExtraBold 800)
- **Subheadline / Location**: 18px – 24px (SemiBold 600)
- **Spec Pills & Badges**: 12px – 14px (Bold 700, Uppercase)
- **Footer / Agent License**: 11px – 13px (Regular/Medium)

## 3. Line Height & Letter Spacing
- Large Headlines: `line-height: 1.1` to keep titles tight and impactful.
- Subtitles & Body: `line-height: 1.4` for maximum readability.
- Status Badges (`JUST LISTED`, `OPEN HOUSE`): `text-transform: uppercase`, `letter-spacing: 1.5px`.

# Graphic Design Principles for Real Estate Marketing

## 1. Visual Balance & Asymmetry
- **Symmetrical Balance**: Used in high-end luxury editorial designs for formal elegance.
- **Asymmetrical Balance**: Used in modern, dynamic property cards where large hero photos are balanced by bold typography and negative space.
- **Radial & Focal Weight**: Eyeball anchors should start at the primary focal point (the property facade or luxury interior) before flowing into price and CTA.

## 2. Contrast & Dominance
- **High Contrast Rule**: Never place light gray typography on white or dark blue typography on black. Text overlays must achieve at least 4.5:1 WCAG contrast ratio over photo backgrounds using gradient scrims or frosted glass containers (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`).
- **Dominance**: Exactly ONE element must dominate every poster—either the hero architectural image, a massive price cut badge, or a high-impact headline.

## 3. Negative Space & Breathing Room
- Avoid crowding edges. Maintain a minimum margin of **60px on 1080x1080 canvases** and **80px on 1080x1920 Story canvases**.
- Group related property specs (`Beds · Baths · Sqft`) into unified pill tags rather than scattering them across the design.

## 4. Visual Rhythm & Harmony
- Repeat brand color accents (e.g. primary brand blue or warm gold) consistently across badges, borders, and CTA buttons.
- Limit each creative to a maximum of **2 font families** (one Serif or Bold Sans for headlines, one clean Sans for specs & body).

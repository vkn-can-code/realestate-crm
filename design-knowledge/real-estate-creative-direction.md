# Visual Hierarchy Guidelines

## Focal Point Order
1. **Primary Anchor**: Dominant Property Hero Photo (Curb Appeal or Architectural Feature).
2. **Secondary Anchor**: Listing Title or Status Badge (`JUST LISTED`).
3. **Tertiary Anchor**: Price Display (`$2,450,000` or `₹78.00 Lakhs`).
4. **Supporting Data**: Bedrooms, Bathrooms, Sqft, Location.
5. **Action Destination**: Call to Action Button / Agent Contact & License info.

---

# Image Composition & Photo Intelligence

## Photo Categories & Assignment Rules
- **Exterior / Facade**: Default choice for Main Hero Photo in Property Listings & Just Listed templates.
- **Living Room / Kitchen**: Secondary supporting photos or hero choice for interior renovation showcase.
- **Pool / View / Waterfront**: Featured badge inset photo for lifestyle templates.
- **Floor Plan**: Explainer infographic inset image for new construction & investment templates.

---

# Social Media Platform Design Rules

- **Instagram**: Emotionally appealing, high aesthetic quality, minimal clutter, strong visual hooks.
- **LinkedIn**: Data-oriented, professional credibility, market metrics, structured headlines.
- **Facebook**: Community-focused, warm tone, clear contact info, open house dates.

---

# Real Estate Creative Direction Standards

- **US Brokerage Norms**: Prominent agent license numbers, brokerage logo, MLS compliance disclaimers, clear price formatting, move-in readiness cues.

---

# Anti-Patterns (What to Avoid)

1. ❌ **Dashboard Bleed**: Avoid placing gray dashboard widgets inside a marketing poster.
2. ❌ **Squished Typography**: Never stretch fonts or use unproportional text boxes.
3. ❌ **Illegible Overlays**: Never place white text over light daylight photos without a scrim or background container.
4. ❌ **Repetitive Card Copies**: Every template family must have a distinct visual grid.

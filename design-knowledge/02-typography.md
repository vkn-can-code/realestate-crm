# 02. Typography Rules & Type Scale

## Typeface Pairings for Real Estate
- **Luxury / High-End Estate**: `Playfair Display` (Serif Title) + `Inter` (Sans-Serif Body & Specs).
- **Modern / Urban Condo**: `Inter` (ExtraBold/Black 800-900) + `Montserrat` (Geometric Badge).
- **Architectural Minimalist**: `Inter` (Medium/Bold 600-700) with generous letter-spacing (`letter-spacing: 0.05em`).

## Type Hierarchy Scale (Based on 1080x1080 canvas)
- **Primary Title**: 40px – 56px (Bold 800/900)
- **Price Tag**: 32px – 44px (ExtraBold 800)
- **Subheadline / Location**: 18px – 24px (SemiBold 600)
- **Spec Pills & Badges**: 12px – 14px (Bold 700, Uppercase, `letter-spacing: 1.5px`)
- **Footer / Agent License**: 11px – 13px (Regular/Medium)

# 03. Layout & Grid Composition Systems

## Composition Frameworks
1. **Full-Bleed Hero Overlays**: High-resolution architectural photo fills 100% width and 70% height with dark gradient overlay for bottom typography.
2. **Split-Screen (50/50 & 60/40)**: Hero exterior image on top/left, supporting interior room photo or stats block on bottom/right.
3. **Card-in-Card Composition**: Outer dark/gradient background frame with an inner frosted glass card (`borderRadius: 24-36px`, `backdropFilter: blur(30px)`).
4. **Architectural Grid System**: Crisp white background with 2px hairline dividers (`#E2E8F0`), strict grid alignments, and generous white space.

# 01. Graphic Design Principles & Craft Directives

## 1. Visual Balance & Rhythm
- **Symmetrical vs Asymmetrical**:
  - Use symmetrical balance for luxury editorial & high-end estate showcases to evoke classic elegance.
  - Use asymmetrical balance for modern architectural & high-energy price drop ads to guide eye movement dynamically.
- **Visual Weight**: Large bold typography or dark high-contrast badges carry visual weight and must be balanced with opposite white space or high-resolution photo focal points.

## 2. Dominance & The One-Focal-Point Rule
- Every creative must have **exactly one primary hero element**:
  1. The Property Exterior Facade (for Just Listed / New Listing).
  2. The Large Price Ribbon (for Price Drop Alerts).
  3. The Circular Agent Portrait (for Meet the Realtor Spotlight).
  4. The 5-Star Quote Mark (for Client Testimonials).
- Never allow secondary text or background badges to compete with the primary hero element.

## 3. Contrast & Readability (WCAG 4.5:1 Standard)
- **Overlay Protection Scrims**: When overlaying text on photographic backgrounds, always apply a gradient scrim (`linear-gradient(to bottom, transparent, rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.95))`) or a frosted glass container (`backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`).
- **Text Readability**: Never place white text over un-scrimmed daylight photos or dark text over dark navy/black containers.

## 4. Spacing & Negative Space
- **Safe Margins**: Maintain a minimum inset padding of 60px on 1080x1080 canvases and 80px top/bottom margin on 1080x1920 Story canvases.
- **Pill Tag Grouping**: Group related specs (Beds, Baths, Sqft) inside unified glass pills rather than scattering unformatted raw text across the image.
